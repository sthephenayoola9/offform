# OFFFORM — Master Product Specification

**Project:** OFFFORM  
**Tagline:** Outside the ordinary.  
**Category:** Creative fashion community with an integrated marketplace.  
**Audience:** Alternative fashion and streetwear enthusiasts, independent designers, emerging ateliers, and creative collaborators.

---

## 1. Product Philosophy & Non-Negotiable Tenets

1. **Community First:** OFFFORM is a creative community with a marketplace attached, never an e-commerce platform with a comment section added as an afterthought.
2. **Inspiration Distinct From Commerce:** A community post does not automatically represent a product for sale. Tagging a designer does not imply endorsement, license, or manufacturing consent.
3. **Subversive Fashion Culture:** Celebrating deconstruction, raw-edge knits, upcycled materials, utilitarian techwear, and artisanal craftsmanship over fast-fashion monoculture.
4. **Human Verification over Black Boxes:** All sellers undergo human review. Automated tools assist with completeness checks but never make unilateral decisions on originality or trustworthiness.
5. **Algorithmic Restraint & Privacy:** No surveillance-driven engagement farming. Members control their recommendation signals and browsing data.

---

## 2. Product Pillars & Architecture

1. **Community & Discovery:** Rich lookbook posts, aesthetic tags, following/discover feeds, likes, comments, and saves.
2. **Independent Marketplace:** Ready-made, made-to-order, customisable garments, and custom commission slots.
3. **Designer Accreditations:** Verified Designer and Approved Seller trust labels backed by human audit.
4. **Bespoke Customisation:** Designer-controlled options (dyes, hardware, distressing, tailored measurements) with live pricing computation.
5. **Creative Collaboration:** Multi-disciplinary briefs connecting designers with stylists, 3D artists, and photographers.
6. **Platform Governance:** Immutable audit logs, hybrid moderation triage, versioned policy acceptance, and privacy-respecting account deletion.
