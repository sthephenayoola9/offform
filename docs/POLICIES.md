## 2. Key Legal & Operational Principles

1. **Creator Ownership:** Uploading creative lookbooks or designs does NOT grant OFFFORM ownership of the creator's intellectual property. OFFFORM holds only a limited hosting and display license.
2. **Inspiration Distinct from Commerce:** Community posts are not commercial products for sale. Being tagged or credited does not imply commercial licensing or manufacturing consent.
3. **Consumer Rights:** Made-to-order and custom garments require honest lead-time disclosure. Cancellation terms respect statutory consumer protection laws in applicable jurisdictions.
4. **Jurisdictional Review:** Pre-launch review required for target regions, including Nigeria's electronic transactions and consumer protection frameworks.
